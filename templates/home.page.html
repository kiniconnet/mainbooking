Welcome to Fort Smythe Bed and Breakfast

Historic charm meets modern comfort

Nestled along the breathtaking shores of the Atlantic Ocean, Fort Smythe Bed and Breakfast offers an unforgettable escape from the ordinary. Our lovingly restored historic property combines timeless elegance with contemporary amenities.

Each morning begins with a gourmet breakfast featuring locally-sourced ingredients, served in our sunlit dining room or on the ocean-view terrace.
